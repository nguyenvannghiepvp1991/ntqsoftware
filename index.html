Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Why is it recommended to unplug at 80% instead of 100%?

Modern Lithium-ion batteries experience the highest chemical stress when charged beyond 80%. Battery university studies show that maintaining your battery between 20% and 80% significantly minimizes wear cycles, potentially prolonging its lifespan by 2-3 years compared to constant 100% saturation.

Does running in the background drain my battery?

Smart Battery is engineered using native Android power-efficiency best practices. It only triggers measurement routines upon plug/unplug broadcast events or when reaching defined thresholds. Typical daily battery consumption is less than 0.5%.

Is the app completely free to use?

Yes! The app is 100% free to download on the Google Play Store. Core charging speed diagnostics, temperature warnings, and full charge alarms are available at zero cost to all users.

How is my personal privacy protected?

The app strictly adheres to Google Play's Data Safety policies. We do not collect contacts, location history, or sensitive personal data. All battery diagnostic operations run locally on your device.
